F1 Legends

1950s

Juan Manuel Fangio

5x World Champion 🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆

The original F1 legend. Fangio dominated the early years of Formula 1, winning 5 championships with 4 different teams — a record that stood for decades.

1980s

Ayrton Senna

3x World Champion 🏆🏆🏆

Widely considered the greatest F1 driver ever. Senna's wet weather mastery, raw speed and emotional driving style made him a global icon beyond motorsport.

1990s

Michael Schumacher

7x World Champion 🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆

The most dominant driver in F1 history for a generation. Schumacher won 7 championships and 91 races with Ferrari and Benetton, redefining what was possible.

2000s

Lewis Hamilton

7x World Champion 🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆

The most successful F1 driver statistically. Hamilton holds records for wins, poles and podiums. Now racing for Ferrari in 2026, chasing an 8th championship.

2010s

Sebastian Vettel

4x World Champion 🏆🏆🏆🏆

Vettel's four consecutive titles with Red Bull from 2010-2013 were built on technical genius and consistency. He became the youngest ever world champion at 23.

2020s

Max Verstappen

4x World Champion 🏆🏆🏆🏆

The current generation's dominant force. Verstappen's aggressive yet controlled style won him 4 back-to-back titles, cementing his place among the all-time greats.
