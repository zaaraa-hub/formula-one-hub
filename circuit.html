Famous F1 Circuits

🇲🇨

Circuit de Monaco

Monte Carlo, Monaco

📏 3.337 km 🔄 78 Laps ↩️ 19 Turns

The most glamorous race on the calendar. Narrow streets, zero room for error and pure prestige make Monaco the crown jewel of F1.

🇮🇹

Autodromo di Monza

Monza, Italy

📏 5.793 km 🔄 53 Laps ↩️ 11 Turns

Known as the Temple of Speed. Monza's long straights produce the highest top speeds of the season, often exceeding 360 km/h.

🇬🇧

Silverstone Circuit

Northamptonshire, UK

📏 5.891 km 🔄 52 Laps ↩️ 18 Turns

Home of the British Grand Prix and birthplace of F1. Copse, Maggotts and Becketts are some of the most iconic corners in motorsport.

🇦🇺

Albert Park Circuit

Melbourne, Australia

📏 5.278 km 🔄 58 Laps ↩️ 16 Turns

The season opener! Melbourne's street circuit sets the tone for the championship, with unpredictable weather and exciting racing.

🇯🇵

Suzuka Circuit

Suzuka, Japan

📏 5.807 km 🔄 53 Laps ↩️ 18 Turns

The only figure-of-eight track on the calendar. Suzuka's 130R corner and the Esses section are beloved by drivers and fans worldwide.

🇦🇪

Yas Marina Circuit

Abu Dhabi, UAE

📏 5.281 km 🔄 58 Laps ↩️ 16 Turns

The season finale venue. Racing under the lights of Abu Dhabi, this circuit has witnessed some of F1's most dramatic championship deciders.
